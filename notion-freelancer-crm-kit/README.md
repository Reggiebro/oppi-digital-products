# Notion Freelancer CRM & Pipeline Kit (Template Spec)

A ready-to-implement Notion workspace blueprint for freelancers/agencies to track leads, clients, projects, invoices, and follow-ups.

## Included
- `template-spec.md` — databases, properties, views, formulas/rollups, and setup steps
- `listing-copy.md` — marketplace listing copy and pricing

## Outcome
One organized system for pipeline, client work, and revenue tracking (no blank-page setup).
