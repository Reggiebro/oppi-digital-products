What the numbers mean

24–30 — take the call. At least one criterion is strong enough to carry the rest, and nothing is a hard no.

18–23 — qualify further. Usually one unknown: budget not stated, or you're talking to a coordinator. Ask before proposing.

Under 18 — decline. Two low scores are fine. Four are a project that will cost more than it pays.

Score honestly and the tool is useful. Score generously and you've built a machine for talking yourself into bad clients.
