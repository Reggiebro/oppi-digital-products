Freelance follow-up emails that get replies

Three short, copy-paste templates for the moments that stall most freelance work. Fill the brackets, keep the tone calm, send.

1) After a proposal (no response, 3–4 days)

Subject: Quick check-in on the [project name] proposal

Hi [Name],

Just checking in on the proposal I sent for [project name] on [date]. Happy to adjust scope or timing if that would make it easier to move forward.

If now isn't the right time, no problem — a quick yes/no helps me plan my schedule.

Best,
[Your name]

2) After a discovery call (next steps)

Subject: Next steps for [project / goal]

Hi [Name],

Thanks again for the call today. Here's what I understood:

- Goal: [goal in one line]
- Scope: [2–3 bullets]
- Timeline: [start → milestone]
- Investment: [price / range]

If that looks right, reply "go ahead" and I'll send the [invoice / agreement / kickoff details]. If anything's off, tell me what to change.

Best,
[Your name]

3) Gentle late-payment nudge

Subject: Invoice [#] for [project] — quick reminder

Hi [Name],

Hope you're well. This is a friendly reminder that invoice [#] for [project] ([amount], due [due date]) is still outstanding.

If it's already on the way, thank you — feel free to ignore this. If there's an issue with the invoice or payment, let me know and I'll sort it out.

Payment details: [link / method]

Best,
[Your name]
