## Source idea
"How freelancers can stop scope creep with a simple change-request rule."

## 1) X post
Scope creep isn’t a client problem. It’s a missing rule.

My rule: every new request gets 3 options
1) paid change request
2) swap for existing scope
3) move to phase 2

Clear. Polite. Profitable.

## 2) LinkedIn post
Freelancers: the fastest way to lose margin is "just one small change."

I use a simple change-request rule:
- check request against agreed scope
- state timeline/cost impact
- offer 3 options: add as paid change, swap, or defer

Clients appreciate clarity. You protect delivery.

## 3) Newsletter snippet
**Subject:** The 3-option rule that stops scope creep

When a client asks for extra work, don't say yes/no immediately. Respond with impact + options. It keeps the relationship good and the project profitable.
