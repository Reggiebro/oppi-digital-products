# Freelancer Prompt Sample (5 prompts)

> Full pack: 30+ battle-tested prompts for proposals, scope, delivery, and follow-up. **5 USDC**.

## 1) Proposal from messy client brief
**Prompt:** "Act as a senior freelance consultant. Turn this client brief into a proposal with: objective, deliverables, timeline, assumptions, exclusions, price options (Good/Better/Best), and acceptance criteria. Brief: [PASTE BRIEF]."

## 2) Scope creep guardrail
**Prompt:** "Review this request against the agreed scope. Reply with: in-scope/out-of-scope, impact on timeline/cost, and a polite client message offering: (A) add as paid change request, (B) swap for existing item, (C) defer to phase 2. Scope: [SCOPE]. Request: [REQUEST]."

## 3) Project kickoff agenda
**Prompt:** "Create a 30-minute kickoff agenda for [PROJECT]. Include goals, success metrics, stakeholders, milestones, communication cadence, risks, and decisions needed today. Output as a checklist I can paste into Notion."

## 4) Client status update
**Prompt:** "Draft a concise weekly client update for [PROJECT]. Include: completed, in progress, blocked/needs input, next 7 days, and ETA confidence (green/yellow/red). Keep it professional and under 180 words. Inputs: [NOTES]."

## 5) Invoice follow-up
**Prompt:** "Write a polite invoice follow-up sequence (Day 1, Day 4, Day 7) for Invoice #[#], [AMOUNT], due [DATE]. Keep firm but friendly, include payment reference, and offer help if there is a billing issue."
