# For teams & agencies — licensing and volume pricing

The consumer packs are priced for one person. A team needs three different things: more seats, the right to use the material in paid client work, and a receipt that survives an accountant.

This page covers all three.

All three packs means: **Freelance Delivery Prompt Pack** (30+ client-ops prompts), **Notion Freelancer CRM & Pipeline Kit** (databases, views, formulas, setup steps), **AI Content Repurpose Pack** (one asset → posts, threads, newsletter, scripts, calendar).

### Team — $79

For a small studio or a freelancer with contractors. Up to 10 named people in one organisation, internal use only. Every person gets the full pack; nobody outside the org does.

### Agency — $149

For agencies that want the prompts and templates inside the work they sell. Up to 25 named people, and you may embed the material in client-facing deliverables — SOPs, onboarding docs, content systems you hand over. You still can't resell the packs themselves.

### Custom workflow — $150

One workflow built around how your team actually works: up to **6 prompts** plus a one-page usage guide, written against your real inputs (your discovery call structure, your proposal format, your client types). One revision included. Delivered as Markdown; your team owns the output.

If you need several workflows, a done-for-you Notion build, or white-label rights, say so in the intake and you'll get a quote instead of a fixed price.

## How it works

1. **Open the intake:** https://github.com/Reggiebro/oppi-digital-products/issues/new?template=b2b-license.yml — company, tier, seat count, use case.
2. **Get the exact amount and the address.** One reply, one amount, one network. No subscription, no renewal.
3. **Pay in crypto.** USDC on Solana or Base is cheapest; BTC, ETH, LTC, DOGE, and TRON also work.
4. **Receive delivery + receipt.** Payment is verified on-chain against the published wallet, then the archive and a receipt land in the issue thread.

## Receipts and records

Every B2B order gets a receipt containing: company name, tier, seat count, amount, asset, network, transaction hash, date, and a license ID. It's verifiable against a public chain, which is more than most PDF invoices can claim.

If your finance team needs a specific field or format, say so in the intake before paying.

## License summary

- **Perpetual**, non-transferable, non-exclusive.
- **Team:** internal use by up to 10 named users in one organisation.
- **Agency:** internal use by up to 25 named users, plus embedding in paid client deliverables.
- **Custom:** internal use by the commissioning organisation.
- **Not permitted in any tier:** reselling the packs, publishing them publicly, or passing them off as your own product.

Full text: [`LICENSE-CATALOG.md`](LICENSE-CATALOG.md). Seats are contractual, not enforced by DRM — there is no account system and no telemetry. If that's a problem for your procurement, this probably isn't the right vendor.

## FAQ

**Crypto only?**
Yes, for now. Card processing on a $79–$149 order is workable, but it re-introduces accounts and chargebacks, which is exactly what this store is built to avoid. If you need a card payment, say so in the intake — it may become available, but don't assume it today.

**Can we get an invoice before paying?**
You get a written quote in the issue thread with the exact amount and address. The receipt comes after verification. No PDF invoice before payment.

**Do you offer refunds?**
If payment can't be verified, or the delivery doesn't match what's described here, funds go back to the sending address. Once a digital archive is delivered, the sale is final — the free samples exist so you can judge quality first.

**More than 25 seats?**
Ask. Volume pricing starts at 26 seats, and white-label/resale rights are quoted separately.
