How checkout works

  1. Send the payment to the matching address below — the right asset on the right network. If unsure, use USDC on Solana or USDC on Base (cheap, reliable).
  2. Open a fulfillment request with your tx hash, network, product(s), and a contact — one click, no account needed.
  3. Get your delivery. Payment is verified on-chain, then the full archive is delivered — instantly after on-chain verification. Full archives are never publicly downloadable; only free samples are.

Questions?
Why crypto-only?No platform fees, no accounts, instant on-chain proof of purchase. Trade-off: automated instant fulfillment.
How do I know I'll get the product?Everything is public and auditable — previews, sales flow, and every fulfillment request tracked in a GitHub issue. Payment is verified on-chain before delivery; free samples show exactly what the full packs look like.
Sent the wrong network or the tx is missing?Open the fulfillment request with your tx hash anyway — we'll trace it. Never send an asset to an address of a different network.
Refunds?If we can't verify your payment or can't deliver what was described, funds are returned to the sending address. After delivery of the full archive, sales are final.
