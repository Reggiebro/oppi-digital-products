# FREE SAMPLE — Notion Freelancer CRM & Pipeline Kit (CRM database blueprint)

## Leads & Clients (CRM) database
**Properties**
- Name (Title)
- Type (Select): Lead, Client, Past Client, Partner
- Status (Select): New, Contacted, Discovery, Proposal Sent, Negotiation, Won, Lost, On Hold
- Source (Select): Referral, Inbound, Outbound, Marketplace, Social, Other
- Service Interest (Multi-select)
- Estimated Value (Number, currency)
- Probability (Select/Number %)
- Expected Close (Date)
- Next Follow-up (Date)
- Last Contact (Date)
- Email / Contact (Text/URL)
- Notes (Text)
- Related Projects (Relation → Projects)
- Related Invoices (Relation → Invoices)

**Views**
- Pipeline Board (Board by Status)
- Follow-ups Due (Table, filter Next Follow-up <= Today or within 7 days)
- Active Clients (Table, filter Type=Client)
- All Contacts (Table)
